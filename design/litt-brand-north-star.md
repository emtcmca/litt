# Litt Brand North Star

Working direction: **Cohesive Command Docket Platform**

This is the baseline for the UI redesign. Litt should feel like an enterprise-grade legal operations platform, not a generic dashboard or a pile of AI-generated cards.

Chosen styling direction: **Ledger House base + Instrument Panel accent**

- Ledger House provides the legal-institutional material: warm paper surfaces, deep green/black proof rail, restrained premium tone.
- Instrument Panel contributes the sharper data treatment: cleaner geometry, tighter borders, crisper operational modules, and a restrained teal proof accent.
- Avoid the Court Terminal electric accent as the default; it is memorable, but too loud for the core product surface.

Chosen type direction: **IBM Plex Sans**

- IBM Plex Sans gives Litt a serious technical-institutional voice without feeling generic.
- Use IBM Plex Sans for product UI, headings, and numerals.
- Pair with IBM Plex Mono or a high-quality system mono for audit IDs, timestamps, state names, routes, and deterministic/system labels.
- Avoid serif display type in the app UI; it pulls the product toward editorial/legal publishing rather than operational software.

## Product Shape

- Left rail: operational scope, closeout sections, and system status.
- Center workbench: attorney decisions ranked by operational pressure.
- Intelligence layer: drilldowns for pressure, audit, and agents inside the workbench.
- Right proof rail: persistent, quiet audit evidence and recent receipts.

## Brand Posture

- Legal-institutional first: credible, restrained, serious.
- Technical second: agentic, deterministic, proof-oriented.
- Avoid: decorative dashboards, marketing surfaces, generic KPI card grids, excessive glow, or terminal cosplay.

## Refinement Priorities

- Tighten typography and spacing rhythm.
- Create distinctive Litt status language and iconography.
- Refine the operational pressure widget into a signature interaction.
- Keep audit trail prominent but calm at startup.
- Make the agentic architecture visible through product evidence, not explanatory clutter.
